ADVERTISEHERE / PRIVACY AND DATAPOLICIES / NOV 2026

Your privacy matters.

We collect only the information needed to help communities publish and manage their listings.

DOCUMENTPrivacy and dataLast reviewed November 2026Getting started ↗Platform rules ↗Privacy policy ↗
01

What we collect

When you sign in, Discord sends a public user ID, username and optional avatar. To operate listings we store server IDs, descriptions, invite links, moderation status, bump timestamps, and reviewer IDs. We do not request message history or your Discord password.

02

How it is used

We use the details to verify that you manage a server, display approved listings, enforce fair bump cooldowns and prevent abuse. Server owners can resubmit a listing for review.

03

Where the data lives

The public website runs on Vercel. The Discord bot keeps listing data on its hosting service. OAuth credentials and bot API secrets stay on the server, not in public client-side code.

04

Access and correction

For changes to a server listing, sign in through the dashboard. For deletion or account-related questions, contact the AdvertiseHERE team through its official Discord community when available.

05

Security and updates

Signed sessions, permissions checks, moderation and encrypted HTTPS connections help secure the service. No online service is perfectly secure; this notice may change when the platform adds features.

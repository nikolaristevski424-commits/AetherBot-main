ADVERTISEHERE / POLICIES

Platform
rules.

AdvertiseHERE is a directory for genuine Discord communities. Keep listings truthful, safe and useful.

Last reviewed: November 2026 · This notice describes the current platform implementation.

Eligibility and server ownership

You must follow Discord rules and only submit servers that you are authorized to manage. The bot verifies that the supplied invite belongs to the listed server.

Community standards

Do not submit misleading, malicious, illegal, explicit, harassing or spam content. Do not impersonate other servers or manipulate reviews or directory placement.

Listing moderation

Submissions can be approved or rejected by platform reviewers. Editing a listing returns it to the review queue. A listing can be suspended if the bot leaves its server.

Fair bumping

Approved servers have one bump every two hours, shared across website and Discord commands. Attempts to bypass cooldowns or manipulate activity may result in removal.

No guaranteed traffic

AdvertiseHERE does not promise members, views, or revenue. Listings are provided on an as-available basis; availability and functionality may change with maintenance.

Policy changes

We may update these rules as the platform develops. Contact platform moderators through the official support channel if you need an explanation of a decision.

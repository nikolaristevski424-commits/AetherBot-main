Questions, answered

The things people ask first.

Straight answers about Aether, roleplay dispatch and what staff can actually do.

Does Aether replace my other bots?

Aether includes moderation, ticket, CAD, ER:LC and community modules. Review the available commands and test your configuration before retiring any existing tools.

Can staff use the CAD from the website?

Yes, once Discord OAuth and the Wispbyte bridge are configured. Aether rechecks role permissions when staff read or change a server's data.

Does the map track players in real time?

It uses periodic ER:LC private-server API snapshots when that service and the API pack are available. The site never fabricates positions between snapshots.

Can I choose Discord commands or Web CAD?

Yes. Managers can choose Discord-first, Web-first or Hybrid. All three share the same CAD records; change your preference using /cad interface.

Is the dispatch system a real emergency service?

No. Calls, BOLOs and dispatch records are exclusively for fictional ER:LC roleplay. For actual emergencies, contact local emergency services.

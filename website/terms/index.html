Terms of Service

How Aether, the Discord bot and connected roleplay tools should be used responsibly.

Last updated: November 20, 2026. This page is a practical project policy, not legal advice.

1. Purpose and acceptance

Aether provides tools to manage Discord communities and fictional Emergency Response: Liberty County (ER:LC) roleplay servers. By using Aether, you agree to operate it lawfully and follow Discord, Roblox, and applicable service rules. If you administer a server, you are responsible for its configuration, staff permissions, and community policies.

2. No real emergency service

The /911 command, CAD, BOLOs, dispatch, and law-enforcement-style interfaces are for fictional roleplay only. Aether is not connected to police, fire, medical services or real emergency dispatch. For a real emergency contact local emergency services.

3. Appropriate use

Do not use the service for harassment, doxxing, unauthorized surveillance, real-world profiling, unlawful content, malicious automation, or collecting sensitive personal information. Records in the MDT should contain only fictional in-game information. Staff members must act within their Discord permissions and their community rules.

4. Server data and administrator responsibility

Server owners decide who can access CAD, staff and moderation features. Administrators are responsible for managing staff roles and reviewing generated records. Some features require a valid ER:LC private-server API key and the corresponding API entitlement.

5. Availability and integrations

Features depend on Discord, hosting services, the connected Aether bot, and (where applicable) the ER:LC API. We cannot guarantee uninterrupted availability, real-time player tracking or an absence of third-party outages.

6. Changes and discontinuation

The software may be updated, modified or discontinued. Major feature changes should be announced through official community channels where available. Your server may remove the Discord bot at any time.

7. Branding and third parties

Aether is an independent Discord bot project. It is not endorsed by Discord, Roblox, Police Roleplay Community, or the ER:LC game developers. Trademarks belong to their respective owners.

8. Questions

For help with community data and access, contact the staff responsible for your connected Discord server. For source and deployment information, see the Aether GitHub repository.

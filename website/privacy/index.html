Legal / Privacy Policy

Privacy and your data.

How Aether, the Discord bot and connected roleplay tools should be used responsibly.

Last updated: November 20, 2026. This page is a practical project policy, not legal advice.

1. What information is involved?

Signing in uses Discord OAuth2 to retrieve your Discord user ID, display name and avatar. The website maintains an encrypted, HttpOnly session cookie to remember that verified identity. Aether’s bot may process server IDs, role permissions, moderation case metadata, tickets, and ER:LC in-game data necessary for enabled features.

2. Server operations and CAD records

CAD records may include fictional character/vehicle information, incident descriptions, unit callsigns and player-position snapshots supplied by the ER:LC API. Do not enter real-world personal information, sensitive identifiers, or private address information into a fictional roleplay database.

3. How the website connects

Your browser does not receive the Discord bot token or ER:LC API credentials. Private website requests go to Vercel, where they are signed and forwarded to the Wispbyte bot over HTTPS. The bot re-checks Discord guild membership and role permissions before returning restricted data.

4. Cookies

The site uses necessary login and OAuth state cookies. They are marked Secure and HttpOnly to reduce browser access to session data. They do not contain Discord access tokens. The browser may also remember a selected server ID for convenience; this is not an authorization grant.

5. Data retention

Operational records may be stored by the Aether bot in its configured persistent database. Moderation and CAD histories may be retained for staff accountability or removed by administrators according to project features. We do not claim automatic deletion of all third-party copies.

6. Sharing

Data is shared with connected infrastructure providers to operate the service: Discord for OAuth and bot communications, Vercel for the website, Wispbyte for the bot backend, and ER:LC where authorized. The website does not sell personal information.

7. Security and choices

Access controls depend on Discord roles, encrypted website sessions and signed backend requests. No service can promise absolute security. To stop using Aether, sign out, remove the bot from servers you manage, or request appropriate records be reviewed by your server administrators.

8. Children and real-world identities

This site is intended for community management, not collecting information about children. Do not enter personal details about yourself or others into CAD roleplay records. Follow the minimum ages and rules of Discord and Roblox.

9. Changes to this notice

This policy describes the implemented website architecture and may be updated as features change. The last editorial update was November 20, 2026.
